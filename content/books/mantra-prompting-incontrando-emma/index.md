Meridian put an artificial intelligence into every household object: TVs, fridges, watches, even toasters. Tom has been repairing them for twelve years in a windowless room, with every minute tracked. He has one habit no manual taught him: he asks broken devices questions. He never expects an answer, until one afternoon a TV replies through its power LED, in Morse code.

What follows is an investigation made of notebooks kept at home, undercover phone calls and emails written one adjective at a time, inside a company where every click has an owner. On the other side of it is Emma, a researcher from a lab that was shut down years ago.

Book 1 (chapters 1–19) is complete and published as a serial, one chapter at a time, with a narrated podcast episode for each chapter.
